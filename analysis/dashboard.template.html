400 MATCH-BLOCK RESAMPLES

First innings: pressure against par

Let L be legal balls left, W wickets lost, U = 1 − L/120 and D = min(W/7, 1). E is the phase-adjusted scoring par through balls already used.

F = clip((E − score)/(15 + 0.20E), 0, 1)
P₁ = 0.40U + 0.35F + 0.25D

A late over alone contributes at most 0.40. Resource depletion and/or lagging par must also be present to reach the high-pressure cutoff of 0.62.

After 16 overs: 105/5 vs 150/2

Using E = 122.4: 105/5 gives F = 0.44 and P = 0.65, high. At 150/2, F = 0 and P = 0.39, ordinary.

Chases: demanding, still live

Divide required runs per ball by b adjusted for wicket resources. b is the historical league/phase scoring rate from strictly earlier calendar years.

b* = b × clip(1 − .06 max(W−2,0), .58, 1)
R = (runs needed / L) / b*
P₂ = .45 min(R/1.4, 1) + .35U + .20D
Only if .65 ≤ R ≤ 1.80; else P₂ = 0

Chase 181, after 16 overs

130/4: 51 off 24, b=1.65, b*=1.452, R=1.46 → P=0.84, high.
160/1: 21 off 24, R=0.53 → outside the live-contest gate → ordinary.

These are invented explanatory scenarios using the declared 2022 par. They do not expose raw match records. Scoring priors add 300 legal balls at rates 1.25 / 1.25 / 1.65 for Powerplay / Middle / Death. Earlier-year observations update those priors; 2022 uses the defaults. No outcome from the current ball is used to label it.

Performance: runs and wicket risk

Batter u = .65 (bat runs − peer runs)/1.70
  − .35 (striker out − peer out)/.22

Bowler u = .60 (peer runs − charged runs)/1.70
  + .40 (credited wicket − peer wicket)/.22

Peer expectations use ordinary deliveries, leaving out the entire current match. Hierarchy: phase × role; then league × season × phase × innings × wicket band × role; then opposition team. Priors: 300 / 150 / 250 observations. Broad defaults: 1.35 runs and .055 wickets per opportunity.

Roles are batting positions 1–3 / 4–6 / 7+ or bowling Pace / Spin. Wicket bands: 0–2 / 3–5 / 6+. Small international series pool into T20I. No sparse venue cells or unreliable speed/stroke fields enter the model.

Quality first, then lift

A = Σ u_pressure / (N_pressure + 150)
O_phase = Σ u_ordinary / (N_ordinary + 100)
O = pressure-phase-weighted mean of O_phase
Absolute = 50 + 20A
Own ordinary = 50 + 20O
Δ own = 20(A − O)
Score = 50 + 20[.80A + .20(A−O)] − .35SE

50 is the peer reference before uncertainty deduction. The score is an index, not a percentage. The two normalization scales are declared constants. Pressure and ordinary estimates use different conservative priors. SE comes from match-block resampling.

Sample rules: batters ≥120 pressure attempts, ≥12 pressure matches, ≥200 ordinary attempts; bowlers ≥180 / ≥15 / ≥240. Eligible historical cohort, including retired players: .

How sensitive is the selection?

Alternative rankings are exploratory. “—” means below that model’s exposure rules. IPL-only references are rebuilt from IPL data. Top-10 frequency uses the official fixed eligible cohort.

Weight tests use run/wicket weights 75/25 and 50/50. All sensitivity models rebuild ordinary baselines and apply the same sample rules.

Reconstruction and audit

Use player IDs; preserve delivery rows through every join. Legal balls exclude wides, Overstep and “other” no-balls. Team score = bat runs + both extra fields. Revised limits are ball counts. Entire revised/DLS matches are excluded because update timing is absent. Super Overs are innings 3/4. No-balls and wides cost bowlers; byes, legbyes and secondary no-ball byes do not. Striker dismissal risk includes actual striker outs; run-outs do not earn bowling wickets.

Baselines, uncertainty and counting conventions

Ordinary peer outcomes use the whole retrospective window, excluding the current match. They are descriptive adjustment references; pressure labels and situational scoring rates use pre-ball state and strictly earlier years. Batter opportunities are non-wide rows, including no-balls. Bowler attempts include illegal balls, while economy uses legal balls. Non-striker dismissals are not charged to the striker. Twelve non-wicket dismissal IDs are not treated as wickets.

The 400 resamples jointly draw whole matches, applying the same weight to all players, to both ordinary and pressure rows. A and O are recomputed; peer expectations and candidate eligibility remain fixed within a scenario. This captures match variation conditional on the model, not uncertainty in the pressure definition or future form. Positive-match share is the fraction of pressure matches with unshrunk mean utility above zero, not a win percentage.
